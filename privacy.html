Spider Lint · Privacy policy · 25 October 2026

Privacy policy

Spider Lint is a Chrome extension that finds accessibility errors on the page you are looking at. This page explains what it can see, what it stores, and what it never does.

Who this covers

This policy is for the Spider Lint browser extension published by Aliaksandr Kavaliou.

What the extension can see

The extension runs only after you click its toolbar icon on a tab. It then injects a panel into that tab and, when you press Check, reads the page to find accessibility issues. It does not run on other tabs, and it does not run in the background on sites you visit.

If you take a screenshot of an issue, Chrome’s captureVisibleTab API captures the visible tab. That image can include personal data that was already on the page. The panel warns you about this before you use the shot.

What is stored, and where

Everything the extension keeps is stored in Chrome’s local extension storage on your device:

  • settings (check level, spider on/off and speed, theme, auto-check options)
  • the list of issues you chose to ignore, grouped by site

Tickets you copy and screenshots you save go to your clipboard or downloads folder, not to us. Closing a tab does not wipe settings. Uninstalling Spider Lint deletes the stored settings and ignore list.

What we never collect

The extension does not send page contents, tickets, screenshots, or settings to us or to any server. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or crash reports.

The check engine is bundled in the extension. It does not phone home. There is no remote code.

Feedback forms

The settings page links to optional forms hosted by Tally if you want to report a bug, suggest an improvement, or send other comments. Those forms are not filled by the extension. If you open one, Tally’s own privacy policy applies to whatever you type there. Nothing is sent from the extension itself.

Permissions

  • activeTab — so the panel can run on the tab whose icon you clicked, and only then.
  • scripting — to inject that panel and run the check on that tab.
  • storage — to keep settings and ignored issues on this device.

The production build does not request host permissions or access to every site.

How to delete your data

Restore ignored issues from the settings page, or remove Spider Lint from chrome://extensions. That deletes local settings and the ignore list. Anything you already copied or downloaded stays on your device until you delete it yourself.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.

Contact
